# ROM catalog provenance

## Pokémon cartridge metadata

The 11 commercial entries contain metadata only. No commercial ROMs were downloaded, bundled, or linked. The 18 variant sizes (in bytes) and SHA-1 digests were checked against the `rom` records in Libretro’s No-Intro DATs, all version `2026.11.14`, pinned at commit `90601bff67dba7057381292f17e08ed2cecbd6dc`.[1][2][3]

The catalog covers clean English retail cartridge dumps whose DAT region includes `USA`. It excludes other regional-only dumps, debug builds, prototypes, unlicensed releases, and digital rereleases (including the Switch revisions of FireRed and LeafGreen). `USA, Europe` denotes one shared image, not two variants. Labels retain the DAT region and revision; the lack of a `Rev` suffix denotes the original release. Platform follows the DAT: Yellow is GB despite its color enhancements; Gold and Silver are GBC despite their GB compatibility.[1][2][3]

`year` is the first US cartridge release year for each title, not the year of a particular revision:

| Platform | Title(s) | US release year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GB | Red and Blue | 1998 [4] |
| GB | Yellow | 1999 [5] |
| GBC | Gold and Silver | 2000 [6] |
| GBC | Crystal | 2001 [7] |
| GBA | Ruby and Sapphire | 2003 [8] |
| GBA | FireRed and LeafGreen | 2004 [9] |
| GBA | Emerald | 2005 [10] |
